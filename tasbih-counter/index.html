Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tasbih counter work?

Tap the big counting area (or press the space bar) once per recitation. The counter tracks the current round against your target — 33, 99, 100 or your own number — then starts a new round automatically and adds to your rounds and lifetime total.

Does it vibrate at the target?

On phones that support vibration, the counter gives a short buzz when you complete a round, so you can count with your eyes closed or the phone in your pocket.

Are my totals saved?

Yes — rounds and lifetime totals are saved in your browser on this device only. Nothing is uploaded. Clearing your browser data resets them.

Which dhikr can I count?

Presets include SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illallah, Astaghfirullah and Salawat (durud). You can also type any custom dhikr or phrase of your own.

How it works

The counter is a digital version of a string of prayer beads. Choose a dhikr from the list — SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, La ilaha illallah, Astaghfirullah or Salawat — or type any phrase of your own, then pick a target: 33, 99, 100, or any number up to 100,000. Each tap of the big button adds one; when the count reaches the target, the phone vibrates (on devices that support vibration), the round is complete, and counting rolls straight into the next round. Your rounds and all-time total are stored in this browser, on this device only.

Worked example

Select SubhanAllah with a target of 33 and tap 33 times: the phone gives a short vibration, the round counter moves to 1, and your total grows by 33. Repeat twice more and you have the familiar 99 — three full rounds — and the total is still there tomorrow if you close the page and come back.

Frequently asked questions

Can I set a target that is not 33, 99 or 100?

Yes — the custom target field accepts any number from 1 to 100,000, which suits a fixed daily wird such as 500 istighfar or 1,000 salawat, where the standard bead targets are too small.

Is the all-time total kept separately for each dhikr?

No — there is one shared running total and one round counter for the page, whichever dhikr you are counting. Switching from SubhanAllah to Astaghfirullah does not reset them; they add up everything you have counted on this device.

My iPhone does not vibrate when I reach the target — is something broken?

No. The vibration uses the Vibration API, which iPhones do not provide to web pages, so on an iPhone the completed round is shown on screen only. On most Android phones the vibration works, provided vibration is switched on in the phone’s own settings.

What happens to my totals if I clear my browser data or change phones?

They start again from zero. The rounds and total live only in this browser’s storage on this device — nothing is kept online or attached to an account — so clearing site data, or moving to a new phone, begins a fresh count.

Does the counter keep going past the target?

Yes. At the target there is a vibration, the round counter steps up by one, and the count restarts from zero automatically — so you can continue to 66, 99 and beyond without pressing anything or losing your place.
