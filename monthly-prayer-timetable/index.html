Frequently Asked Questions

Can I print this timetable for my masjid or home?

Yes. Use the Print button — the page switches to a clean black-and-white table with the city, month, method and Asr setting in the header, ready for a noticeboard.

Which month and year can I generate?

Any month you choose, past or future. Times are calculated day by day for the exact coordinates of the selected city (or your custom coordinates).

Why do the times shift by a minute on some days only?

The sun’s path changes unevenly through the year — around the equinoxes times can move two minutes a day, while near the solstices they may stay the same for several days.

Does the table show Sehri and Iftar?

Sehri ends at Fajr and Iftar is at Maghrib, so the Fajr and Maghrib columns are your Sehri and Iftar times. For Ramadan specifically, the Ramadan Timetable tool presents exactly those two columns day by day.
