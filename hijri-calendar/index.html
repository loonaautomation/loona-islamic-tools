Frequently Asked Questions

Which calendar does this Hijri calendar follow?

The Umm al-Qura calendar, the calculated calendar used officially in Saudi Arabia and built into your device’s international date system. Local moon-sighting calendars can differ by a day.

Can I view any Hijri month and year?

Yes — pick any month and Hijri year, or step month by month with the arrow buttons. Each day cell shows the Hijri day with its Gregorian date underneath.

Why does a Hijri month sometimes start mid-week with greyed days?

The greyed cells are the tail of the previous Hijri month, shown so the week rows stay complete — the Gregorian date under each day keeps everything unambiguous.

How do I find the Gregorian date of a specific Hijri day?

Open that Hijri month here and read the small Gregorian date inside the day’s cell. For single-date conversion in either direction, the Hijri Date Converter on Loona Calculators is linked from our homepage.

How it works

The calendar is built by converting dates with the browser’s built-in Umm al-Qura calendar: the page finds the Gregorian day on which the chosen Hijri month begins, lays its 29 or 30 days into a week grid that starts on Monday, and prints the Gregorian date inside every cell, so the two calendars can be read side by side. Arrows step month by month, and the month and year fields jump straight to any Hijri month — the year field accepts years from 1300 to 1700.

Worked example

Jumada al-Ula 1448 is a 30-day month: it begins on Monday 12 October 2026 and ends on Tuesday 10 November 2026. In the grid, Saturday 7 November 2026 sits as the 27th of Jumada al-Ula — one cell carrying both dates at once.

Frequently asked questions

Why can this calendar differ by a day from the one announced locally?

This is the calculated Umm al-Qura calendar, whose month lengths are fixed in advance by astronomical rules. Where the start of a month is announced by sighting the moon instead, a cloudy horizon or a late sighting can shift the whole month by a day — the difference is expected, and local announcements govern religious observances.

How do I find when Ramadan starts in a given year?

Jump to month 9 (Ramadan) of that Hijri year: the first cell of the month is 1 Ramadan, with its Gregorian date printed in the same cell. In the calculated calendar, Ramadan 1448 begins on Monday 8 February 2027.

Why do Hijri months alternate between 29 and 30 days?

A lunar month is about 29.5 days, so whole-day calendars alternate long and short months to keep pace with the moon. In the Umm al-Qura calendar the length of each month is fixed ahead of time by its rules for Makkah — Jumada al-Ula 1448, for example, has 30 days, and the month after it has 29.

Does the calendar mark Eids or other special days?

No — the grid shows dates only, without event markers. The two Eids are still easy to locate, because their Hijri dates never move: Eid al-Fitr is always 1 Shawwal (month 10), and Eid al-Adha is always 10 Dhul Hijjah (month 12).

Why does the week in this calendar start on Monday?

The grid follows the Monday-first week layout used in most printed calendars in Pakistan and Europe. It changes only the arrangement of the cells — every day still carries its correct weekday, Gregorian date and Hijri date.
