Frequently Asked Questions

When does Ramadan start in Brunei in 2027?

On the Umm al-Qura calendar, 1 Ramadan 1448 falls on Monday 8 February 2027, and the month is expected to end on Monday 8 March 2027, with Eid al-Fitr on Tuesday 9 March 2027. The confirmed start in Brunei depends on the local moon sighting and may be a day earlier or later.

What are the Sehri and Iftar times in Bandar Seri Begawan during Ramadan 2027?

How long is the fast in Brunei in Ramadan 2027?

In Bandar Seri Begawan the shortest expected fast is 13 hours 8 minutes (on 8 February 2027) and the longest is 13 hours 12 minutes (on 5 March 2027), measured from Fajr to Maghrib.

Which calculation method does this Ramadan timetable use?

The times use the Muslim World League method (Fajr 18 degrees, Isha 17 degrees) with Shafi Asr — the convention most widely followed in Brunei. Because fasting begins at Fajr and ends at Maghrib, the Asr setting does not change the Sehri or Iftar times. Local masjids may add precautionary minutes to Sehri end.
