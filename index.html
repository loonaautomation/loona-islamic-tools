Frequently Asked Questions

Are these Islamic tools free to use?

Yes. Every tool on Loona Islamic Tools is 100% free, with no sign-up and no account. Open any page and use it right away.

How are the prayer times calculated?

Times are calculated in your browser with the standard solar (PrayTimes) method used by mosques and apps worldwide. The default method is the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi (Fajr 18°, Isha 18°) with Hanafi Asr, and you can switch method, Asr school and city on every prayer page.

Is my location or personal data uploaded anywhere?

No. All calculations run 100% on your device. Your chosen city, settings and tasbih totals are stored only in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to a server.

Which Hijri calendar do you use?

The calendar pages use the Umm al-Qura calendar built into your device (via the internationalised date system). Actual month starts can differ by a day in some countries because of local moon sighting.

Why does Asr time differ between Hanafi and Shafi settings?

The schools use different shadow factors: Asr begins when an object’s shadow equals its height (factor 1, Shafi) or twice its height (factor 2, Hanafi). Both options are provided, and the page always shows which one is in use.
